Across the full study sample, mean recorded gross revenue per listing-month was €2,630.81 in 2023 and €2,328.13 in 2024, a change of -€302.68 (-11.5%); mean gross revenue per calendar night was €84.86 versus €75.10 (8,552 listing-months in 2023; 12,620 in 2024). These are observational comparisons of recorded gross revenue, not profit or evidence that price changes caused revenue to change. For LA 2028 hosts, the comparison highlights that higher nightly prices alone do not guarantee higher gross receipts; costs, availability, booking timing, and local demand matter, and Paris does not identify an optimal LA price.
