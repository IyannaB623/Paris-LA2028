# Revenue field and analysis notes

The source contains `revenue` and `native_revenue`. All rows report `currency=EUR`; the revenue fields differ, with a median `revenue / native_revenue` ratio of 1.079. We use `native_revenue` as EUR consistently with the price-field assumption. This is an explicit assumption because the exact Kaggle file does not expose readable field-level definitions. An analogous published AirROI past-calendar schema describes revenue as total generated and native revenue as the amount in native currency. Source: https://www.airroi.com/data-portal/markets/paris-france (past calendar rates schema).

`available_days` was not used in the denominator. Here `available_days` and `unavailable_days` sum to calendar days and occupancy is mechanically aligned with unavailable days; they should not be mistaken for booked nights. The per-calendar-night measure is `native_revenue / days_in_month`.

Missing `native_revenue` is not imputed; a missing value is excluded from the corresponding revenue comparison, and zero values are retained. Revenue per calendar night is `native_revenue / days_in_month`, never divided by `available_days`. The primary matched comparison has 2,352 listings with all four months still present after applying the pooled price cutoff, and averages July and August within listing-year. The earlier untrimmed full-sample and matched results are retained in `revenue_supplementary_full_sample.csv`. Comparisons are per listing, not market totals, and are observational.
